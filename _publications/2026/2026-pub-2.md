abstract: >-
  This paper studies bilevel optimization with a constrained lower level, where the lower-level model is unavailable and the response mapping is locally Lipschitz but possibly nonsmooth, as in Stackelberg games with black-box followers. Bi-ZOL (Bilevel Zeroth-Order Learning) keeps the exact upper-level partial gradients and uses zeroth-order sampling only to estimate the response Jacobian. It converges to a stationary point with a quantified bias, and experiments on tracking problems show improved performance over standard zeroth-order smoothing.
